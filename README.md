## Featured projects

### Field Shift Egypt

A bilingual farm decision-support prototype for Egypt. It brings together user-provided farm details, historical climate data, satellite imagery context, and crop references to help people explore crop options and compare rotation scenarios.

- Choose a location across Egypt and add the farm information you know.
- Explore climate summaries, crop traits, and rotation comparisons.
- Review data sources, missing information, and uncertainty alongside results.
- Built with Python, Flask, JavaScript, SQLite, Leaflet, and NASA POWER, GIBS, and CMR data services.

[Live app](https://field-shift-egypt.vercel.app) · [Source code](https://github.com/mohammedhamada911-creator/field-shift-egypt) · [Arabic project guide](https://github.com/mohammedhamada911-creator/field-shift-egypt/blob/main/docs/field-shift-idea-guide-ar.pdf)

> Field Shift is an exploratory tool. Its rotation scores are not yield, profit, or water-use predictions. NASA does not endorse the project.
